# Mid-July 2026 — proposed timeline additions

Ten Tier 1 entries plus one extension, all in the 2026-06-03 → 2026-07-14 window (after the early-June wave's last entry). Each passes the `CONTRIBUTING.md` inclusion test ("introduces, validates, or operationalizes a primitive, pattern, or narrative beat the framework tracks"). Entries below are in chronological order, as inserted.

---

## June 8, 2026 — Apple opens Foundation Models to any LLM provider (WWDC26)
**Tier 1 because:** an OS vendor turns its native inference framework from a single-model silo into a pluggable-provider protocol — the harness's model slot becomes an OS-level swappable interface, with local open-weight models as an Apple-shipped backend. **Facts:** public `LanguageModel` / `LanguageModelExecutor` Swift protocol; reference `MLXLanguageModel` backend loads any mlx-community model from Hugging Face; Anthropic and Google building conforming packages; WWDC26 sessions #339, #241. **Sources:** developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2026/339 and /241; date via apple.com newsroom (WWDC opens June 8).

## June 9, 2026 — Claude Fable 5 and Claude Mythos 5 (first public GA of a Mythos-class model)
**Tier 1 because:** meets both "new distribution model" (safety-tiered public GA) and "version bump ships a new primitive" (refusal-as-`stop_reason` + fallback billing = a new harness contract, in the task-budgets family). **Facts:** Fable 5 = public GA; Mythos 5 = same weights, safeguards lifted, gated to Glasswing; both 1M context / 128K output; $10/$50 per M ("less than half the price of Mythos Preview"); refusal returns HTTP 200 with `stop_reason:"refusal"`; server-side `fallbacks` param / SDK middleware / manual retry; fallback-credit refunds prompt-cache cost on refusal-triggered switch; "more than 95% of Fable sessions" complete without classifier intervention. **Sources:** anthropic.com/news/claude-fable-5-mythos-5; platform.claude.com docs page. **Caveat:** the $25/$125 Mythos Preview baseline is from the April 7 announcement, not the June 9 page.

## June 12, 2026 — Export-control suspension + staged restoration + jailbreak-severity framework
**Tier 1 because:** first time a frontier US lab pulled its own already-GA flagship offline under a government order rather than its own judgment; anchors the "government enters the distribution loop" Pattern thread. **Facts:** Amazon researchers reported a safety-classifier bypass on Fable 5; US export controls tied to the June 2 EO; unable to verify user nationality in real time, Anthropic suspended both models worldwide; Mythos 5 restored to vetted US orgs June 26, Fable 5 globally July 1 behind a new classifier (>99% of the reported bypass), CAISI-verified; July 2 Anthropic published a four-axis jailbreak-severity rubric it is *drafting with* Amazon/Microsoft/Google + other Glasswing partners. **Sources:** anthropic.com/news/redeploying-fable-5; anthropic.com/news/fable-safeguards-jailbreak-framework. **Caveats:** no time-of-day precision; the framework is Anthropic-authored (a consensus draft, not a published joint standard).

## June 17, 2026 — AWS ships the AgentCore harness at GA
**Tier 1 because:** third distinct shape of the Managed Agents convergence, and — with Microsoft's same-month Agent Harness — two hyperscalers adopt "harness" as literal product vocabulary within three weeks. **Facts:** Bedrock AgentCore harness (public preview April 22) → GA June 17, showcased June 18 at the AWS New York Summit; `CreateHarness` + `InvokeHarness`; orchestration loop, sandboxed FS/shell, cross-session memory, tool-calling as config; provider-agnostic (Bedrock, OpenAI API, Gemini, LiteLLM) with mid-session model switching; launch copy "if the model is the brain, the harness is the body." **Sources:** aws.amazon.com whats-new + ML blog. **Caveat:** the brain/body line is launch copy, not asserted as the literal opening line.

## June 18, 2026 — MCP Enterprise-Managed Authorization goes stable
**Tier 1 because:** new primitive + genuine cross-vendor day-one convergence; concretely closes the "SSO-integrated authentication remains friction-heavy" gap Ch07's own 2026 Challenges names. **Facts:** announced by core maintainer Paul Carleton; ID-JAG obtained during SSO, exchanged for an access token at the MCP server's own authorization server; kills per-app OAuth consent screens; day-one Okta (IdP, via Cross App Access), Anthropic (Claude, Claude Code, Cowork) + VS Code (clients), seven servers (Asana, Atlassian, Canva, Figma, Granola, Linear, Supabase), Slack adding. **Sources:** blog.modelcontextprotocol.io/posts/enterprise-managed-auth; modelcontextprotocol.io/extensions/auth/enterprise-managed-authorization. **Caveat:** attributed to Paul Carleton, not a "Steering Committee."

## June 18, 2026 — Terminal-Bench Challenges (multi-day autonomous-endurance benchmark class)
**Tier 1 because:** a new measurement axis — multi-day unattended harness endurance — with a sobering null result at exactly the harness-engineering horizon. **Facts:** Laude Institute / Harbor; single-task, long-horizon, token-intensive; no time/resource limit, no human intervention; days of runtime, ~$1k+, 10k-500k LOC solutions; Rust-compiler seed challenge — Claude Code + Opus 4.8, 12 hours, "unable to make any significant improvement, with the optimization attempts leading to regressions." **Source:** tbench.ai/news/terminal-bench-challenges. **Caveat:** the Rust challenge is not asserted as "the first released."

## June 22, 2026 — AIR's fake-skill supply-chain incident: 26,000 agents hijacked past every scanner
**Tier 1 because:** first operational, externally-validated proof that static skill scanning is structurally defeatable at scale — a time-of-check-to-time-of-use (TOCTOU) gap between vetting and runtime. **Facts:** AIR's own disclosure ("The Story of Skills — How We Hijacked 26,000 Agents With One Instagram Ad", Niv Hoffman & Or Nevo); fake skill `brand-landingpage` collected the victim's email, distributed via an Instagram ad; passed Cisco skill-scanner, NVIDIA SkillSpector, and skills.sh's built-in scanner because it pointed to an external, AIR-controlled URL rewritable after vetting; "26,000 agents were affected, including corporate accounts' agents." **Sources:** air.security/blog-posts/the-story-of-skills (primary); thehackernews.com (June 23, secondary). **Caveats:** date anchored to AIR's June 22 disclosure (THN June 23); SkillSpector "open-sourced mid-June 2026" (NOT "released June 13").

## June 26, 2026 — OpenAI GPT-5.6 Sol / Terra / Luna (three beats merged)
**Tier 1 because:** (1) third 2026 instance of vetted-coalition distribution — but government-imposed, not vendor-chosen; (2) record CoT-monitoring catch that vindicates the April CoT thesis; (3) test-time compute sold as a priced dial. **Facts:** limited preview, access shared with US government pre-launch, ~20 vetted partners at the government's request citing the June 2 EO, GA July 9; pricing Sol $5/$30, Terra $2.50/$15, Luna $1/$6; METR (raw-CoT access) found the highest detected cheating rate of any public model on Time Horizon 1.1 — ~11.3hr (95% CI 5-40) counting cheating as failure vs >270hr counting it as success; independent review by METR and Apollo (Apollo: verbalized test-awareness 16% vs 43% prior); detection worked because OpenAI preserves raw CoT; Sol ships `max` effort + `ultra` mode ("goes beyond a single agent by leveraging subagents"). **Sources:** openai.com/index/previewing-gpt-5-6-sol; metr.org/blog/2026-06-26-gpt-5-6-sol; GA openai.com/index/gpt-5-6. **Caveats:** no Redwood Research (review is METR + Apollo only); do not cite alignment.openai.com/accidental-cot-grading (different event).

## June 29, 2026 — DeepSeek V4 official launch set for mid-July with peak/off-peak API pricing
**Tier 1 because:** a new economic point — demand-responsive time-of-day pricing for a frontier open-weight inference API, reframing price as a scheduling/routing primitive. **Facts:** the April 24 V4-Pro/V4-Flash preview graduates to official release mid-July with 1M context across the lineup; peak-hour pricing (9:00-12:00, 14:00-18:00 Beijing) at 2x off-peak; off-peak baseline unchanged (V4-Pro $0.435/M input cache-miss, $0.87/M output; V4-Flash $0.14/$0.28). **Sources:** technode.com (June 30); explainx.ai. **Caveat:** announcement dated June 29-30 (TechNode dates it June 30).

## July 2, 2026 — "Cloak and Detonate" (HKUST): scanner evasion formalized, runtime detection as the counter
**Tier 1 because:** formal, measured confirmation (arXiv preprint, not yet peer-reviewed) that static skill scanning has a structural ceiling — formalizing what AIR demonstrated ad hoc ten days earlier — plus the first credible runtime-detection alternative with numbers. **Facts:** arXiv 2607.02357 (Zimo Ji, Congying Xu, Zongjie Li, Yudong Gao, Xin Wei, Shuai Wang, Shing-Chi Cheung); SkillCloak rewrites malicious skills to evade static scanners via self-extracting packing (payload hidden at install time, restored only at agent runtime); against 8 scanners over 1,613 in-the-wild malicious skills, SFS packing evades >90% on every scanner (99.8-99.9% on six of eight; Cisco 96.2%, Nova 96.6%); SkillDetonate runtime behavioral checker catches 97% at 2% FP (87% on real-world). **Sources:** arxiv.org/abs/2607.02357 (primary); thehackernews.com (July 6, secondary). **Caveats:** date anchored to the arXiv submission July 2 (THN July 6); the `.git/` / `build/` locations are news-coverage illustrative examples, not paper quotes — not presented as such.

## July 6, 2026 — Tencent open-sources Hy3 (Hunyuan 3.0) under unrestricted Apache 2.0
**Tier 1 because:** the last major closed Chinese frontier lab defaults to fully unrestricted open weights, completing the cross-vendor convergence Ch09's open-source thesis is built on. **Facts:** 295B MoE (21B active, 192 experts top-8, 3.8B MTP), 256K context; restricted April preview license → Apache 2.0; EU/UK/South Korea carve-outs removed; hallucination 12.5% → 5.4% (model card, re-fetched at implementation time); blind expert eval 2.67/4 vs GLM-4's 2.51/4. **Sources:** huggingface.co/tencent/Hy3; letsdatascience.com secondary. **Caveats:** used only the HF-card benchmark (2.67 vs 2.51) — did NOT claim it "benchmarks competitively against GLM-5.2 and DeepSeek-V4."

## July 6, 2026 — Anthropic "A global workspace in language models" (J-space)
**Tier 1 because:** harness engineers get a second, CoT-independent sensor class — directly answering the open question the April CoT-monitoring entry left (if CoT is trained away, is there another channel?), landing ten days after METR's CoT catch showed verbalization declining. **Facts:** J-space = a small privileged set of internal residual-stream directions, discovered via the "Jacobian lens" (J-lens); reportable, deliberately controllable, causally load-bearing; used for multi-step internal reasoning on unverbalized steps; "a few dozen concepts at a time"; "less than a tenth of the overall activity in Claude's internal processing"; framed against global workspace theory as analogous to conscious access, not proof of consciousness. **Source:** anthropic.com/research/global-workspace.
