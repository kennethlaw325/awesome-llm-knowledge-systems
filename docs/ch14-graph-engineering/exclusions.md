# Exclusions and corrections — Graph Engineering wave

Claims that circulated with the late-July 2026 research window but did not survive primary-source verification. Each was excluded from, or corrected before, drafting. Listed neutrally with reasons, so the exclusions are on the record without re-circulating the unverified specifics.

## Excluded (unverified; appear nowhere in the guide except this file)

- **"Zep 2.0" launch (reported April 2026).** A Graphiti-based Zep 2.0 (bi-temporal edges, automatic fact invalidation, P95 300ms retrieval) is described by low-quality aggregator blogs only; no primary Zep announcement was located. Excluded entirely; only the gh-verified getzep/graphiti repo facts are eligible for the guide.
- **LazyGraphRAG landed in the microsoft/graphrag OSS library.** The official discussion thread targeted Q1-Q2 2026 for the integration, but shipping could not be confirmed as of July 2026. The guide may cite the v3.x release line (v3.1.1, July 18, gh-verified) but not a LazyGraphRAG-landed claim.
- **DeepSeek V4 GA (rumored ~July 20).** Multiple secondary outlets claimed a mid-July GA; the official DeepSeek changelog's newest entry remained the April 24 V4 preview at check time. Excluded as unconfirmed.
- **The X trending topic as a source.** A trending-topic page ("AI Builders Shift from Loops to Graphs for Smarter Agents") surfaced in search but is not a citable source; the Steinberger post's own URL in the research record was an x.com trending link, not the post. Neither URL appears in the guide; the post is cited only via fetched secondary sources.

## Dropped by the chapter author (weaker material, not used)

- **A bare view count for the catalyst post.** The traceable figures are snapshots at different moments: 575K within hours (explainx), roughly 800K over two days (Tony Bai), 2.6M within two days (36kr). A fourth figure, 2.9M, circulated in the research record but could not be traced to any source and is not used. The chapter reports the spread, attributes each figure to its source, and states no single number as fact.
- **A single tweet date.** Every source that dates the post dates it **July 18**; the "July 17-18" range used in headings is a US-time hedge, not a documented split between sources, and the chapter says so in-text. An earlier draft asserted a source split; that assertion was itself unsourced and has been corrected.
- **KuCoin / crypto-news flash coverage** of the term. Surfaced in search, unfetched, low evidentiary value. Not mentioned.
- **The zhihu / runoob "CN pipeline maturity" detail** (loop-engineering explainers on those platforms as a proxy for what awaits graph engineering). Unfetched search-result evidence; the chapter keeps the pipeline observation qualitative without naming those platforms.
- **CSDN and alphalab.site pieces as sources.** Two CSDN 智能体开发者社区 titles and a Traditional-Chinese explainer surfaced in Chinese search but were not fetched. The chapter cites them only as titles-in-search-results with an explicit not-independently-fetched flag, and they do not appear in the Sources list.

## Corrected (right framing used)

- **Coinage.** Steinberger's post does not contain the phrase "graph engineering"; no single person coined it. The chapter attributes the compound to the response-essay wave, patterned on prompt/context/harness/loop engineering.
- **36kr-only attributions.** The participation of Geoffrey Huntley, Boris Cherny, Addy Osmani, and Luis Catacora in the discourse rests on 36kr's article alone; the chapter says so in-text where it names them.
- **LangChain's dual role.** Their July 22 post both legitimizes the term (listing it after the four prior "engineerings") and disputes its novelty; the chapter carries it explicitly as both the strongest adoption signal and the strongest skeptical source, not as one or the other.
- **explainx date.** The guide self-dates July 18 with updates through July 26; cited as "July 18, updated July 26" rather than a single date.
